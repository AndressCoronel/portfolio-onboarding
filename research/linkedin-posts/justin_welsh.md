# Justin Welsh - LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Core Philosophy
The Hub and Spoke Model. Treat LinkedIn as a spoke that sends high-quality traffic to the hub (newsletter).

### Key Posts / Top Performing Structures
1. **The "Contrarian Take" Post:**
   - Hook: "Everyone tells you to do X. They are wrong. Do Y instead."
   - Body: Simple bullet points showing why his method saves time and makes money in B2B.
   - Call to Action: Link to his systematic newsletter in the comments.

2. **The "Process Breakdown" Post:**
   - Hook: "How I grew to $X M ARR with zero employees."
   - Body: Step-by-step breakdown using extreme formatting (lots of white space). Explains his content syndication process.
   - Takeaway: Systems beat motivation.

3. **The "Listicle" Post:**
   - Hook: "10 tools I use daily to run a 7-figure B2B business."
   - Body: Curation of extremely useful, specific SaaS tools.
   - Takeaway: High value per word. Drives heavy bookmarking and shares.
