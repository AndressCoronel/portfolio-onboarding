# Jasmin Alić - LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Core Philosophy
Conversational copywriting and heavy comment-led relationship building. "LinkedIn is a networking event, not a billboard."

### Key Posts / Top Performing Structures
1. **The "Formatting Secret" Post:**
   - Hook: "People don't read on LinkedIn. They skim. Here's how to format for skimmers."
   - Body: Demonstrates the "1-line, 3-line, 1-line" spacing rule.
   - Takeaway: Readability is just as important as the message.

2. **The "Engagement Strategy" Post:**
   - Hook: "I spend 2 hours a day commenting. It's my #1 lead generation source."
   - Body: Step-by-step breakdown of how leaving valuable, paragraph-long comments on bigger accounts drives inbound.
   - Takeaway: Inbound doesn't just come from your own posts.

3. **The "Conversational Tone" Post:**
   - Hook: "Read your B2B copy out loud. If you sound like a robot, rewrite it."
   - Body: Practical examples of translating stiff corporate jargon into everyday language.
   - Takeaway: People buy from people, even in B2B SaaS.
