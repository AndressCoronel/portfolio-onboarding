# Lara Acosta - LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Core Philosophy
Personal branding mixed with tactical growth strategies. Showing personality while over-delivering on actionable tips.

### Key Posts / Top Performing Structures
1. **The "Before & After" Post:**
   - Hook: "I used to get 10 likes per post. Now I average 1,000. Here’s the exact pivot I made."
   - Body: Emphasizes the importance of profile optimization and fixing bad hook structures.
   - Takeaway: Transparency builds immense trust.

2. **The "Tear Down" Post:**
   - Hook: "Why your B2B SaaS posts are getting zero traction (and how to fix it in 5 mins)."
   - Body: Analyzes a generic corporate post vs. a highly personalized founder-led post.
   - Takeaway: Corporate speak is dead. Real talk wins.

3. **The "Cheatsheet" Post (Document / PDF):**
   - Hook: "I audited 100 viral LinkedIn posts. I mapped out the 5 hooks that always work."
   - Body: High-design PDF carousel. Extremely actionable.
   - Takeaway: Visual assets increase dwell time drastically.
