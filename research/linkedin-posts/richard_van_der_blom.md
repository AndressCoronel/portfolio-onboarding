# Richard van der Blom - LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Core Philosophy
Data-backed social selling. Using algorithmic insights to maximize reach and drive enterprise B2B sales.

### Key Posts / Top Performing Structures
1. **The "Algorithm Report Teaser" Post:**
   - Hook: "We analyzed 1M+ LinkedIn posts. Here is what the algorithm wants right now."
   - Body: Shares 3-4 key data points (e.g., text limits, best times to post) and creates a powerful FOMO effect.
   - Takeaway: Proprietary data is the ultimate authority builder.

2. **The "Social Selling vs. Cold Pitching" Post:**
   - Hook: "Cold outreach is dying. Social selling is taking over."
   - Body: Compares the ROI of building a presence versus sending spammy DMs.
   - Takeaway: Education shifts buyer paradigms.

3. **The "Employee Advocacy" Post:**
   - Hook: "Your company page will never out-perform your CEO's personal profile."
   - Body: Case study showing how activating employees on LinkedIn drives massive organic impressions for SaaS brands.
   - Takeaway: Founder/Employee-led growth is non-negotiable.
