# Matt Barker - LinkedIn Content Strategy

## Core Philosophy
Punchy, fast-paced copywriting. Eliminating fluff to respect the B2B buyer's time.

### Key Posts / Top Performing Structures
1. **The "Delete This" Post:**
   - Hook: "If your landing page says 'We streamline your workflow', you are losing money."
   - Body: Shows the bad copy vs. the good copy ("We save your team 5 hours a week").
   - Takeaway: Sell the outcome, not the mechanism.

2. **The "Template" Post:**
   - Hook: "Steal my 3-step framework for writing a LinkedIn post in 10 minutes."
   - Body: Provides the exact fill-in-the-blank template he uses for B2B ghostwriting.
   - Takeaway: Give away the secrets for free, people will still hire you to do the work.

3. **The "Common Sense" Post:**
   - Hook: "Stop overthinking your content strategy. Just answer the questions your sales team gets every day."
   - Body: A simple, highly relatable rant about how B2B companies overcomplicate content.
   - Takeaway: Relatability drives comments.
